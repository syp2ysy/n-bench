EgoConseq-Bench

GOAL: EgoConseq-Bench

Evaluate whether MLLMs can bind visible egocentric geometry, ego body footprint, and a hypothetical local action into body-conditioned swept-volume consequences from RGB plus question only, within the currently visible local space.

showcaseworkflow phase
5designed categories
50accepted R2R cases

Design Rule

The taxonomy must be derived from the goal: test whether an MLLM grounds ego pose, body footprint, action, and visible depth in navigation scenes. It must not reuse T1-T5 as the final taxonomy.

Statusfrozen
Source DatasetR2R
Reference PolicyUse prior spatial, affordance, navigation, and collision benchmarks only as methodology motivation; do not copy their taxonomies, do not keep legacy T1/T2/T3/T4/T5 ids or titles, and do not expose depth, pose, overlays, or navmesh to the evaluated model. Every category must avoid scene-answer leakage, use finite visible-evidence horizons where needed, enforce answer and conditional balance, and report scan/episode coverage to reduce majority, radius-only, action-only, and scene-prior shortcuts.
