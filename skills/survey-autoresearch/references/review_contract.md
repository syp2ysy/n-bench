# Review Contract

The final review should teach a field, not display a workflow.

Required qualities:

- newcomer can understand the core object and vocabulary;
- expert can see a defensible taxonomy or framework;
- every major claim is evidence-grounded;
- benchmark sections explain capability, protocol, metric, baseline, and confounder;
- method sections compare mechanisms, assumptions, results, and limitations;
- open problems follow from evidence gaps and propose concrete research moves;
- conclusion returns to the central thesis and does not introduce new scaffolding.

Repair routes:

- weak paper understanding -> repair mechanism cards;
- unsupported claim -> repair evidence spans or downgrade language;
- shallow benchmark section -> build benchmark dossiers;
- taxonomy feels like buckets -> build method-family dossiers and argument graph;
- article reads like artifacts -> rewrite from article plan and move logistics to appendix;
- repeated or generic prose -> run article quality repair.
